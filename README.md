# Autotrader

Autotrader is the UK's largest digital automotive marketplace, providing a suite of REST APIs through the Autotrader Connect platform. The APIs enable technology partners and retailers to integrate vehicle data, stock management, valuations, retail metrics, and deal management directly into their systems.

## APIs

- **Vehicles API** - VRM/VIN lookups, MOT history, vehicle features
- **Taxonomy API** - Makes, models, generations, derivatives
- **Stock API** - Dealer forecourt inventory management
- **Images API** - Vehicle photography CDN storage and management
- **Search API** - Consumer-facing vehicle advert search
- **Valuations API** - Current UK market vehicle valuations
- **Vehicle Metrics API** - Supply, demand, and pricing intelligence
- **Future Valuations API** - Predicted vehicle valuations
- **Historic Valuations API** - Historical valuation data
- **Deals API** - Deal lifecycle management
- **Part Exchange API** - Trade-in vehicle management
- **Co-Driver API** - AI-generated descriptions and image ordering
- **Finance API** - Motor finance quotes and applications (Beta)
