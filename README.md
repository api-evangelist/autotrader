Autotrader is the UK's largest digital automotive marketplace, providing REST APIs through the Autotrader Connect platform for vehicle listings, dealer stock management, VIN and VRM lookups, vehicle valuations, retail metrics, taxonomy, images, deals, and consumer-facing search. The platform enables technology partners and retailers to integrate automotive data and marketplace functionality into their systems in real time.

**Areas:** Automotive · Vehicles · Marketplace · Dealers · Listings · Valuation · VIN · VRM · Inventory · Car Buying · Car Selling

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Autotrader: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
